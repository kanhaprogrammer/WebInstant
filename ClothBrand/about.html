Our Story

From a dream to a fashion movement

2020

The Beginning

ClothBrand was born from a vision to redefine Indian fashion. Our founder, Aditya Sharma, a fashion enthusiast and entrepreneur, started with a small boutique in Mumbai, offering curated styles for the modern Indian.

2021

First Collection Launch

We launched our first collection of premium shirts and jackets. The response was overwhelming, confirming that Indians were looking for fashion that combines quality, style, and comfort.

2022

Expanding Our Reach

We expanded our collection to include ethnic wear, accessories, and footwear. Our online store launched, allowing us to reach customers across India who value premium fashion.

2023

Community & Style

We started our style blog and social media community, educating customers about fashion trends and styling tips. Our influencer collaborations began, making us a go-to brand for fashion inspiration.

Today

Growing Movement

Today, ClothBrand serves over 50,000 customers across 50+ cities. We're not just a fashion brand—we're a community dedicated to helping people express themselves through style.
